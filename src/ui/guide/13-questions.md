# Common questions

These are the questions new users actually ask, many of them at the moment something looks broken. Most of the time it is working as designed, and the answer is one sentence. Each answer links to the section that explains more.

Press **F1** from anywhere to open Help, and use **Search the guide** at the top left to find a word in any chapter.

## Questions about saving and getting things back

### Do I have to press Save anywhere?

Almost never. Your pages save themselves within a second of typing; the top of the page says **Saved**. The scene plan, a record's name and its notes save when you click away from them. A new fact on a record is kept when you click **Save** on its small form, which is part of adding it. Novel setup saves every choice as you make it.

The one exception is **Settings**, where most sections wait for **Save settings** at the bottom of the window. See [Save settings, and sections that take effect right away](#save-settings-and-sections-that-take-effect-right-away).

### I changed a setting and it went back. Is Settings broken?

No. You most likely left the page without pressing **Save settings**. The line beside that button tells you: "You have changes not saved yet" means not saved. Make the change again and press the button. See [Save settings, and sections that take effect right away](#save-settings-and-sections-that-take-effect-right-away).

### I deleted a scene by mistake. Is it gone?

No. Deleting moves it to **Deleted things**. Click the **Story Trail** tab, then **Deleted things**, then **Restore** beside the scene. A deleted chapter comes back whole with **Restore with its scenes**. See [Deleted things: restoring what you removed](#deleted-things-restoring-what-you-removed).

### I clicked Undo, but the old row is still in the Story Trail. Did it work?

Yes. The trail never erases history. Look at the original row: its text is now struck through and it says "Not in effect." A new row at the top, starting **Undid:**, has **Put it back** in case you change your mind. See [Put it back, and what is in effect now](#put-it-back-and-what-is-in-effect-now).

### Undo says something depends on it. What should I do?

Read the notice. It tells you what later work relies on the change. **Undo anyway** goes ahead; **Leave it** cancels. See [When Undo asks first](#when-undo-asks-first).

### Can I get back yesterday's version of a scene?

Yes. Open the scene, click **Earlier versions** at the bottom of the scene plan, click yesterday's time, and **Restore this version**. Today's text stays in the list. See [Earlier versions of a scene](#earlier-versions-of-a-scene).

### Earlier versions shows only one version, or none. Is it saving?

It is. Your typing is saved within a second, always. A separate *version* is made after a pause of about twenty seconds, when you leave the scene, and when you close the program. A short burst of typing makes one version. See [Earlier versions of a scene](#earlier-versions-of-a-scene).

### Can I open one of the automatic backup files?

Not from inside Story Canon. Backups are whole-library safety copies for a computer that fails completely. To keep a copy of one novel you can open yourself, use **Save an archive of this novel** in Settings, and bring it back from the Library. See [Backups and archives](#backups-and-archives).

### How do I move a novel to a new computer?

Two ways. For a one-time move, save an archive in Settings and bring it in from the new computer's Library (see [Getting a novel back from an archive](#getting-a-novel-back-from-an-archive)). To keep writing on both, set up sharing (see [Your other computers: writing on two machines](#your-other-computers-writing-on-two-machines)).

## Questions about finding and capturing

### Where did my captured thought go?

To **Questions and ideas**, at the top of the Story Brain's list: a thought to **Captured thoughts**, a question to **Questions I have**, an idea to **Things and ideas I'm thinking about**. Unsorted thoughts also show on **Where was I** under **Thoughts waiting to be sorted**. A thought captured inside a scene also waits in that scene's plan, under **Attached to this scene**. See [Where captured thoughts wait](#where-captured-thoughts-wait).

### What is the difference between "Just a thought" and "An idea I am thinking about"?

A thought is unsorted: you capture it without deciding what it is. An idea is something you already know you might use. When in doubt, choose **Just a thought** and sort it later with **Make it a question** or **Make it an idea**. See [The three kinds of capture](#the-three-kinds-of-capture).

### Search did not find something I said in ChatGPT. Why?

Search only sees what is inside Story Canon. Bring the chat in first, from the Conversations tab. See [Bringing in chats from Claude and ChatGPT exports](#bringing-in-chats-from-claude-and-chatgpt-exports).

### Search shows notes with codes like CX-002, "T L22330" and "NEEDS YOLANDA". What are they?

They came in with a novel built from detailed planning files, such as *I Declare War*. The codes are reference numbers from those files, and "NEEDS YOLANDA" was the planner's note to herself. Click a code to see what it points to. Your own novels will not have them unless your files did. See [Reading a search result](#reading-a-search-result).

### There are 176 results. How do I narrow them?

Use the row above the results: **Your pages**, **Story records**, **Conversations** or **Documents**. See [Narrowing search results](#narrowing-search-results).

## Questions about the collaborator

### The collaborator says "not set up." What do I do?

Add your Claude API key in **Settings**, under **The collaborator**, click **Use this key**, and tick **Use the collaborator for** this novel. If it says "off for this novel," the key is there; only the tick is missing. See [Turning the collaborator on](#turning-the-collaborator-on).

### Does the collaborator read my manuscript?

Only if you allow it, and only one scene at a time. Tick **Also share the prose of a scene when I ask about that scene**, then ask from the scene with **Ask about this scene**. Otherwise it sees your plans and records, never your pages. See [The beat sheet and records the collaborator can see](#the-beat-sheet-and-records-the-collaborator-can-see).

### Does the collaborator cost money?

Yes, a little per question, billed by Anthropic to your own API key. Longer questions and answers cost more, and **Opus** costs the most per question. Everything else in Story Canon is free to use without it. See [Choosing which Claude answers](#choosing-which-claude-answers).

### Does anything get sent anywhere without me knowing?

No. Nothing leaves your computer until you have set a key, ticked **Use the collaborator for** this novel, and clicked **Ask**. There is no background sending. See [Use the collaborator for this novel](#use-the-collaborator-for-this-novel).

### The collaborator raised a question I never asked. Did it change my story?

No. A question the collaborator raises is a suggestion. It waits in **Suggestions waiting** in the Story Brain, apart from your own questions, until you **Accept** it, say **Not yet**, or say **No**. See [Suggestions from the collaborator, and how they wait](#suggestions-from-the-collaborator-and-how-they-wait).

### I said "sounds good" to an idea in the chat. Is it in my story now?

No. Agreeing in a conversation decides nothing. Accept the idea on purpose in **Suggestions waiting**, or select its words and use **Add as a possibility**. See [Answering a suggestion in Suggestions waiting](#answering-a-suggestion-in-suggestions-waiting).

### My messages are signed with someone else's name.

Your messages are signed with **Your name** in Settings. A book brought in from someone else's planning arrives with their name there. Type yours and press **Save settings**. See [Your name](#your-name).

### I started from "Ask about this scene," but "Focus on…" shows nothing chosen.

That is fine. The scene goes along with every question anyway; the focus list holds records, and a page is not a record. If the scene has a card, the card is chosen for you. See [Focusing a conversation on one record or scene](#focusing-a-conversation-on-one-record-or-scene).

### Why did the collaborator give a short answer when I wanted a full one?

Check two things: the way to ask (choose **Teach it** or **Critique** instead of **Talk it through**) and your guidance level (**Quiet** answers only what you asked). With the door closed, questions from a scene are always quiet. See [The six ways to ask](#the-six-ways-to-ask) and [Guidance levels: Quiet, Advisory and Deep](#guidance-levels-quiet-advisory-and-deep).

## Questions about conversations

### Why are there stars and pound signs in my imported chat?

ChatGPT and Claude use them for bold text and headings. Story Canon keeps every character exactly as written, so they stay. See [Reading a conversation](#reading-a-conversation).

### My chat says it has hundreds of behind-the-scenes steps. Did something go wrong?

No. Those are the other app reading files, running tools and taking notes to itself. They are kept but hidden, because they are not conversation. Click **Show them** to see them faintly. See [Behind-the-scenes steps, out of sight](#behind-the-scenes-steps-out-of-sight).

### Harvest shows "None." under every heading. Is it broken?

No. Harvest gathers what *you* caught. It never picks things out for you. Go back to the conversation, select the words that matter, keep or file them, and Harvest fills up. See [Harvest: gathering what a conversation produced](#harvest-gathering-what-a-conversation-produced).

### I selected some words but no bar appeared.

Your selection probably ran across two messages. Select within one message only. See [Selecting words in a chat](#selecting-words-in-a-chat).

### I chose "Add as a possibility" on ChatGPT's words, and it arrived as a Suggestion, not Developing.

That is on purpose. Words an AI said always arrive as a suggestion, so an AI's idea never becomes yours just by being selected. Accept it when you are ready. See [Add as a possibility](#add-as-a-possibility).

### If I bring in the same Claude or ChatGPT export twice, will I get duplicates?

No. Chats you already brought in say **already here** in the list. See [Bringing in chats from Claude and ChatGPT exports](#bringing-in-chats-from-claude-and-chatgpt-exports).

## Questions about importing and exporting

### I imported a planning document and nothing went in. Why?

Passages go in only where a heading names a record you already have (or a beat). Look under **Staying in the document**, choose a record and a blank for each passage you want, tick it, and click **Add the checked passages**. See [The planning document preview](#the-planning-document-preview).

### The Done list says "kept whole, every passage searchable." Did the import fail?

No. A planning document is always kept in **Your documents**, whether or not any passage went into a record. See [The words in the import lists](#the-words-in-the-import-lists).

### Where will my imported chapters go?

After your last chapter. The preview says so exactly, such as "the first one becomes chapter 2." Move chapters later in the Writing Room. See [The manuscript preview](#the-manuscript-preview).

### Where did my exported file go?

In the Windows program you choose the place, starting in Documents, Story Canon, Exports. After saving, the notice says "Saved to" and the full path, with **Show it in its folder**. See [After Export: the check and where the file goes](#after-export-the-check-and-where-the-file-goes).

### My [[notes for later]] printed in my export.

Notes for later print exactly as typed. The summary above the **Export** button warns you how many are in the text. Answer or delete them first, or leave them on purpose in a working copy. See [Before you export: the summary above the Export button](#before-you-export-the-summary-above-the-export-button).

### Can I export only some chapters?

Yes. Under **The manuscript**, choose **Only the chapters I pick**, then untick the ones to leave out. See [Choosing chapters to export](#choosing-chapters-to-export).

### My plotting sheet is full of tiny numbers.

There is no target length, so the sheet measures against the few words drafted so far. Set **Target length in words** in Settings and export again. See [Your plotting sheet](#your-plotting-sheet).

### Which do I use, "Novel percentages (Brody)" or "Screenplay proportions (Snyder)"?

For a novel, use **Novel percentages (Brody)**. The Snyder numbers come from a screenplay of 110 pages, scaled to your book. See [Your plotting sheet](#your-plotting-sheet).

## Questions about settings, drafts and the book's shape

### The "Kept for you" marker shows the wrong name, or no name.

It uses your name from **About you**, or the first word of **Your name** in Settings. Fix either one and press **Save settings**. See [The marker for what is yours to write or decide](#the-marker-for-what-is-yours-to-write-or-decide).

### What is the difference between "Mine to write" and "Mine to choreograph"?

Each names the kind of work waiting for you: writing it, staging the movement, timing the reveal, and so on. The collaborator drafts none of them. See [Other markers for work you keep](#other-markers-for-work-you-keep).

### The circle beside my scene does not match its words, or what Settings says.

The circle follows the scene's **Status**, not its word count. Empty before the first step marked **Counts as drafted**, half at that step, full after it. (The grey line in Settings describes this differently; the binder works as described here.) See [Counts as drafted, and the circles in the binder](#counts-as-drafted-and-the-circles-in-the-binder).

### My book says "Plotting and planning" and "First draft" at the same time.

Both are right. The stage is where the whole book stands; the draft is which pass of the pages you are on. A book can be in planning while its first draft is open. See [Stages and drafts: two different questions](#stages-and-drafts-two-different-questions) and [Drafts: keeping a whole version of the book](#drafts-keeping-a-whole-version-of-the-book).

### I finished my draft too early. Can I take it back?

Yes. On the Drafts page, click **I finished first draft too soon**, then **Reopen it**. Your writing is not touched. See [I finished the draft too soon](#i-finished-the-draft-too-soon).

### Why can't I click "Save this method"?

It needs a name. Fill in **Name** at the top. See [Making a method, step by step](#making-a-method-step-by-step).

### I made a method, but my novel does not use it.

Methods belong to you; each novel chooses its own. Tick it in Novel setup under **My storytelling toolkit**. See [Turning your method on for a novel](#turning-your-method-on-for-a-novel).

### My other computer's work is not showing up.

Check three things: both computers chose the same shared folder, OneDrive has finished syncing, and you clicked **Bring it in** on the notice above the page (if **Ask me before bringing in work from my other computer** is ticked). The other computer shares a few minutes after you stop making changes, and when it closes. See [Day to day on two computers](#day-to-day-on-two-computers).

### A note I dictated on my phone never arrived.

The novel has to be open in Story Canon, and the note arrives about a minute after the file stops changing and syncs. Look under **Thoughts waiting to be sorted** on Where was I. See [Notes from the road](#notes-from-the-road).

### Story Brain says "Beats 21," but the Board shows 15.

The Story Brain counts every beat record, including the steps inside beats, such as Stasis = Death inside Setup and the five Finale steps. The Board shows the 15 main beats. See [Smaller steps inside a beat: Stasis = Death and the Finale](#smaller-steps-inside-a-beat-stasis-death-and-the-finale).

### My planned scenes do not show in the Writing Room. Where are they?

Scene cards you planned but have not written wait in the Writing Room's binder, below your chapters, under **Planned, not written yet**, each with **Write it**. Writing one gives it a page in the right place. See [Planned, not written yet, and Write it](#planned-not-written-yet-and-write-it).

### The pink "Return to Writing" ribbon is not showing.

It appears once you have something to return to: after you have been on a page in the Writing Room, or when the last scene you opened has words on it. In a brand-new book with nothing written, there is nothing to return to yet. See [The Return to Writing ribbon](#the-return-to-writing-ribbon).

### Where is the help for the screen I am on?

Press **F1**, or click the **Help** tab at the bottom of the right edge. Many screens also have a **Learn more** link that opens Help at the right section. On a record, **Read the full chapter in Help** opens the reference chapter for that kind of record, such as [Characters: every field](#characters-every-field).
