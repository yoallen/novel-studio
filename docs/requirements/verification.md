## Errors

### E1. Anthony narrating in FIRST PERSON is recorded as her decision. It is not. (Substantive)
- **IDs:** IDW CA-005, PV-003, SC-011a, CX-016; C R-C-043 Evolution ("She later confirmed Anthony's chapters are first person (A15-018, S7-103)").
- **What's wrong:** All of these mark "Anthony narrates in first person" as Established and attribute it to her. Her only statement is T L19957: "This chaptet would definitely be Anthony's POV. otherwise we would not be able to capture the beauty of anthony's torment." She says POV, not grammatical person. "First person" first appears in ChatGPT's reply (T L19966-19995: "because Anthony is narrating in first person"), and the 10-15 archive then wrote it under an "AUTHOR DIRECTION" label (A15 L22: "Anthony's chapter/POV is FIRST PERSON"). IDW SC-011a even puts "first-person" in front of her quoted words. The Bible says "Decided. Kevin narrates in first person, and the point of view alternates between Kevin and Anthony" (SB L289) and "He narrates the novel in first person" (SB L60). It never gives Anthony's person.
- **Evidence:** T L19955-19958; T L19960-19995; A15 L22; SB L60, L289.
- **Correct reading:** Established: the call-in chapter is in Anthony's POV; POV alternates Kevin/Anthony (Bible). Anthony's grammatical person is Proposed (ChatGPT plus an archive mislabel), and Needs Yolanda. CX-016's resolution "Alternating first person stands (Established)" must be reversed. The archive's AUTHOR DIRECTION label is an instance of the archive promoting assistant wording (the same risk IDW CX-041 describes).

## Overall assessment

The four registers are broadly faithful to the source. Her decisions are quoted accurately in almost every sampled case. The "replaced" claims sampled (five-dollar bill, chapter/page citations, genres moved into Phase 2, Anthony not dry, Kevin's job loss in the past, Anthony's job loss in-story, MVP rejected) all reflect her actually changing her mind. Skimmed proposals (S2-179 to S2-194, the 16:26 integrity list) are correctly kept as Unanswered, and ChatGPT spec claims about her preferences (pie/bar charts, "capture first, organize later", "panther at heart") are correctly flagged as not in her words.

The most serious defect is E1. An assistant inference ("Anthony narrates in first person") reached Established through the A15 archive's AUTHOR DIRECTION label. That is the exact failure the registers warn about. E2 is a contradiction inside and across registers about a fact she never stated. M1 drops one of her explicit 10:14 decisions. E3 attributes ChatGPT's phase numbering to her. Register B has enough broken cross-references (E4) that it needs a reference pass before a builder relies on it. Fix E1, E2, E3 and M1 before the registers are used as the build baseline.
