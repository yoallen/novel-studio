# I Declare War (IDW): Consolidated Story Data

Novel: *I Declare War*, a comedic novel by Yolanda Allen.
Purpose: the story data for this project in her novel-planning program. Every fact carries a status and its provenance.
Compiled: 2026-10-16.

## How to read this file

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Established | She decided it. Sources: her own words in the conversation, a "Decided." passage in her Story Bible, or an "AUTHOR DIRECTION" / "AUTHOR CORRECTION" / "AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED" item in the archives that matches her own words in the conversation. |
| Developing | A working version she is still shaping: her "what if", "I think", "just a thought", the Bible's unlabeled "What it is" plans, or an archive "DEVELOPING" item. |
| Unanswered | An open question, including the Bible's "Over to me / My move / My turn / Now I write / Still open" slots. |
| Illustrative | An example, never canon. Covers the Bible's "One way I could play it, not a decision yet", "Weak:/Strong:" examples, sample dialogue, and assistant lines labeled as play. |
| Proposed | An AI suggestion (ChatGPT, archive "EDITORIAL POSSIBILITY", or poster interpretation) that she has not accepted. Items she only answered with a blanket "continue" or "agree" also stay here. |
| Set aside | She replaced it, or it was an AI error she corrected. It is kept only for history and is never canon. |

### Source keys
- **SB-###**: Story Bible v5 (2026-10-13), using the register IDs in `section-docs-story.md`. "SB L##" means the line in the Bible text.
- **A14-### / A15-###**: Character Breakthrough Archives of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15. These are editorial reconstructions by ChatGPT, not verbatim records. "A14 L##" / "A15 L##" means the archive line.
- **PF-###**: the poster foundation JSON, manifest and QA files (generated 2026-10-14, derived from SB plus her overrides).
- **XL-###**: her 2021 BS2 beat-sheet spreadsheets. These are general conventions, not IDW canon.
- **S1-### to S8-###, S8E-###**: register items for the design conversation, `section-1.md` to `section-8.md`. "T L####" means the line in the conversation transcript (`90955321-Story_Map_Planning_FULL_with_code.md`). S8E means the edited-versions file.
- **SP-### / HV-###**: the Phase 2 spec and the Human Vision brief, which ChatGPT wrote. Used only for the story facts they state.

### Weighing rules applied
1. Her own words in the conversation carry the most weight.
2. A later statement replaces an earlier one only when she actually changed her mind.
3. A later archive, poster, summary, example or proposal never overrides her decision. Leaving something out never cancels it.
4. Possibilities coexist, and additions build on earlier facts.
5. What she established about sources (T L20960, L18816): newer character work she developed and agreed outranks older example text in the Bible (Kevin's 10-14 work over the Bible's example Kevin Lie), and the Bible's Anthony entry "really isn't accurate anymore." Her own later words outrank anything ChatGPT wrote.
6. Not established: ChatGPT's three-tier order (her latest decisions, then the Oct-14 archive, then the Oct-13 Bible; S7-137, S8-001, T L21076-21084) is an Unanswered proposal. Her "Please do so" (T L21090) answered the offer to develop Anthony's arc, not the ranking. The Oct-15 archive's own source policy (A15-062) was never put to her. Neither is used here as a rule. The archives are used as evidence only where they match her words in the conversation.

### Fact IDs
Each fact has an ID: PT- (premise/theme/tone), CK- (Kevin), CA- (Anthony), CF- (Francine), CS- (supporting cast), RL- (relationships), BT- (beats), SC- (scenes and set pieces), CL- (clues/plants/payoffs), KN- (knowledge), TS- (timelines/setting), PV- (POV), DL- (dialogue), OQ- (open questions), CX- (conflicts).
