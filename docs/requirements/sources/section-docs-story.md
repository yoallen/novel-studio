### 0.3 Relationship values

These follow the brief:
- REPLACES: newer text clearly supersedes older text.
- ADDS TO: newer text builds on older text.
- ALTERNATIVE TO: both options stay alive.
- UNCLEAR: the relationship can't be settled from the text.
- Additional values used here:
  - "BASE": the item is the earliest statement and nothing later touches it.
  - "CONSISTENT WITH": a later source restates the item without change.
  - "CONFLICTS WITH": a disagreement that needs author resolution. This is used mainly for PF and for SB's own internal inconsistencies.

### 0.4 Precedence rule stated by the sources themselves
A15 L72: "The October 13 Story Bible remains the baseline for established plot architecture; October 14 Character Breakthrough Archive has priority for the revised Kevin working history and precise lines; October 15 discoveries here further develop Anthony, the feud, and the ledger. All source documents remain separate. Do not silently promote an assistant suggestion or a speculative formulation to established canon." (Convention, Established.)

### 1.8 Part Three: The Integrated Beat Sheet (SB)

Labeling note for import: in SB Part Three, each beat follows the same pattern.
- "Also known as" gives the cross-framework names.
- "What this beat really does" and "How to make it land, and how it fails" are craft teaching, recorded here as Conventions.
- "What it is" is an unlabeled working plan for IDW, often hedged with "maybe" or "perhaps". It is recorded as Developing.
- "Weak:/Strong:" examples are recorded as Illustrative.
- The prefix "One way I could play it, not a decision yet:" is attached to the *hand-off prompt* rather than to a story example.
- The "Over to me / My move / My turn / Now I write / Here's where I take over" slot is empty in every beat. It is recorded as Unanswered.

These two workbooks are Yolanda's own Save the Cat ("BS2" = Blake Snyder Beat Sheet) breakdowns. They predate IDW (2021) and define how she models beats for **any** novel. They should drive the program's general beat model; they are not IDW story canon. Every non-empty cell of both workbooks was read, with formulas and cached values compared. Both workbooks contain **no formulas**: every number is a typed constant.
